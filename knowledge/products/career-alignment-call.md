# Career Alignment Call

A guided conversation service from [[3doshas]] where clients discuss their [[career-alignment-blueprint]] patterns and get guidance on next moves. Step 2 in the three-step career alignment journey.

## Overview

offered_by:: [[3doshas]]
follows:: [[career-alignment-blueprint]]
precedes:: [[one-on-one-coaching]]

An optional live session to discuss Blueprint results, translate leadership traits and Blueprint insights into practical steps toward the next role or promotion.

## What It Includes

- Review of [[career-alignment-blueprint]] results
- Discussion of dosha patterns and their career implications
- Guidance on next moves and career transitions
- Practical action steps
- Assessment of fit for [[one-on-one-coaching]]

## Format

- Available as a free 15-minute introductory call
- Extended sessions available for deeper exploration
- Conducted online via Zoom
