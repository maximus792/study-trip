# Student Mentorship & Coaching Program

A coaching program offered by [[3doshas]] designed for students and early-career professionals, including mentorship circles and career readiness sessions.

## Overview

offered_by:: [[3doshas]]
based_on:: [[3doshas-methodology]]

Tailored for students entering the job market or early in their careers. Uses the [[career-alignment-blueprint]] to help students identify their natural strengths and align with career paths.

## Program Components

- Mentorship Circle sessions (weekly online, Fridays and Saturdays)
- Student-focused career coaching
- Interview preparation
- Internship and job placement guidance
- Entrepreneurship Youth Program (ages 10+)
- Monthly community events
