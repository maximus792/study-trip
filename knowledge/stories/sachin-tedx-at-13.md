# Sachin's TEDx Talk at Age 13

The story of [[sachin-syal]] becoming one of the world's youngest TEDx speakers at age 13, delivering "How To Find Your Passion and Know Your Purpose" at [[tedx-youth-warm-springs]].

## The Story

protagonist:: [[sachin-syal]]
event:: [[tedx-youth-warm-springs]]

From selling candy in elementary school to making five figures on eBay by age 13, Sachin discovered his passion for entrepreneurship young. When he was anxious about what his passion was, he found the art of entrepreneurship. This personal journey became the foundation of his TEDx talk, where he encouraged other young people to find their passion early.

## Narrative Arc

1. Elementary school: Started selling candy to classmates
2. Middle school: Built an eBay reselling business, earning five figures by age 13
3. TEDx: Channeled his experience into a talk about finding passion and purpose
4. This trajectory ultimately led to co-founding [[3doshas]] with his family

## Why It Matters for 3Doshas

This story demonstrates the Syal family's commitment to helping people find alignment early in life — a core principle of the [[3doshas-methodology]]. It also established [[sachin-syal]]'s public speaking credibility, which later supported his role as CMO and his appearances on [[bloomberg]] and [[apple-tv]].
