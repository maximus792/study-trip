# Sachin Pitches 3Doshas on Bloomberg TV at 15

The story of [[sachin-syal]] becoming the world's first youngest finalist ever on [[bloomberg]] TV's [[2-minute-drill]] pitch show at age 15.

## The Story

protagonist:: [[sachin-syal]]
event:: [[2-minute-drill]]
company:: [[3doshas]]

[[sachin-syal]] represented [[3doshas]] as a global finalist on the "2 Minute Drill" pitch show, airing on both [[bloomberg]] and [[apple-tv]]. He pitched to a panel of self-made multi-millionaire and billionaire judges including Janice Bryant Howroyd, Rory Cutaia, Jayson Waller, and David Meltzer.

## Significance

- Youngest finalist in the show's history
- Brought national television exposure to [[3doshas]]
- Led to the [[ja-worldwide]] Impact Award
- Demonstrated that the [[3doshas-methodology]] resonated with high-level business leaders
