# A Family-Founded Coaching Company

The story of how the Syal family co-founded [[3doshas]], each member bringing unique expertise to create an integrated coaching platform.

## The Story

protagonist:: [[sumeet-syal]]
protagonist:: [[nitu-syal]]
protagonist:: [[sachin-syal]]
protagonist:: [[sujay-syal]]

Inspired by their Indian heritage, the Syal family co-founded the 3Doshas Coaching Institute, incorporating principles from Yoga & Ayurveda to help others enhance their careers and personal growth.

## Each Member's Contribution

- **[[sumeet-syal]]** (CEO): 30+ years of tech leadership, executive coaching methodology, corporate relationships
- **[[nitu-syal]]** (COO): 30-year biotech career, developed the [[career-alignment-blueprint]] methodology with scientific rigor
- **[[sachin-syal]]** (CMO): Youth perspective, media visibility through [[bloomberg]]/[[apple-tv]], podcast network via O'ChakDe Show
- **[[sujay-syal]]** (CTO): Built the entire platform, owns AI strategy and data analytics

## Why It Matters

The family structure gives [[3doshas]] authenticity — the methodology isn't just professional, it's personal. Each family member embodies different dosha energies, and their collaboration demonstrates the very team-alignment principles they teach to corporate clients.
