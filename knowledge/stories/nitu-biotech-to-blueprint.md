# From Biotech Executive to Blueprint Creator

The story of [[nitu-syal]]'s transition from biotech leadership at [[genentech]] and [[amgen]] to creating the [[career-alignment-blueprint]] at [[3doshas]].

## The Story

protagonist:: [[nitu-syal]]

After 30 years leading at the intersection of science and wellness — with senior roles at [[genentech]], [[amgen]], and Cadence Health — [[nitu-syal]] channeled her scientific background into developing the [[career-alignment-blueprint]].

## The Bridge

Nitu's unique contribution was bridging her scientific training (UC Berkeley Molecular Cell Biology) with the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda. The Blueprint methodology is described as "scientifically measurable" — reflecting her biotech background's emphasis on evidence and data.

## Why It Matters

The Blueprint's credibility rests partly on Nitu's scientific credentials. Having a UC Berkeley-trained molecular biologist design the diagnostic tool gives it a legitimacy that pure wellness-based approaches might lack in corporate settings.
