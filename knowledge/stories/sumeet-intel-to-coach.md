# From Intel VP to Executive Coach

The story of [[sumeet-syal]]'s journey from Intel Vice President & General Manager to founding [[3doshas]] and returning to Intel as an external executive coach.

## The Story

protagonist:: [[sumeet-syal]]
origin_of:: [[3doshas]]

After 30+ years in global tech — rising from engineering at Silicon Valley startups to VP & GM at [[intel]] — [[sumeet-syal]] made a dramatic career pivot. Inspired by his Indian heritage and the ancient wisdom of Yoga & Ayurveda, he founded [[3doshas]] with his family in 2020.

## Career Arc

1. **Himalayas to California**: Grew up in India's Himalayas, moved to California
2. **Early hustle**: Managed a McDonald's as a teen, worked at Fry's Electronics
3. **Engineering**: Started in Silicon Valley startups acquired by industry leaders
4. **Singapore expansion**: Spearheaded Intel's Asia-Pacific regional expansion
5. **Intel leadership**: Rose to VP & GM of Global Sales & Marketing
6. **Education**: Stanford executive training, UC Berkeley-Haas coaching certification
7. **Founding 3Doshas**: Combined tech leadership experience with Ayurvedic heritage
8. **Full circle**: Returned to Intel as an external coach

## Why It Matters

The "returning to Intel as a coach" narrative is particularly powerful — it shows that the same organization that shaped Sumeet's corporate career now benefits from his coaching expertise. It validates the [[3doshas-methodology]] at the highest corporate levels.
