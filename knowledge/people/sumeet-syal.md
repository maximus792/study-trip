# Sumeet Syal

CEO & Executive Coach at [[3doshas]]. Entrepreneur, tech executive, and educator with 30+ years of global experience across large corporations and Silicon Valley startups.

Sumeet is the founder and CEO of [[3doshas]], an award-winning coaching platform. He designs and hosts high-impact leadership workshops for premier Silicon Valley companies including [[google]], [[intel]], and [[palo-alto-networks]], partnering with executives to build high-performing teams and resilient corporate cultures.

## Corporate Career

- Served as Vice President & General Manager at [[intel]] Corporation, leading global sales, marketing, and technology alignment for Fortune 500 enterprises, hyperscalers, and AI service providers.
- Started career in engineering at Silicon Valley startups that were acquired by industry leaders.
- Moved to Singapore, spearheading Intel's Asia-Pacific regional expansion and mastered Asian business practices & marketing as an expat.
- Closed high-stakes negotiations, securing multi-billion-dollar deals with tech giants including [[google]], Apple, Dell, and HP.
- Consistently ranked among top Intel executives for transforming teams into high-performing, resilient organizations.
- Product management leadership for Intel's go-to-market strategy for smartphone & wearable products; insights featured in CNET, TechCrunch, and Business Insider.

## Education

- Dual MBA from UCLA-Anderson and National University of Singapore
- BSc in Computer Engineering from Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo
- Stanford-trained executive in Leading Change & Organizational Development
- UC Berkeley-Haas Certified Executive Coach

Developed the [[3doshas-methodology]] — a science-based coaching framework inspired by his Indian heritage. Incorporates principles of [[vata]], [[pitta]], and [[kapha]] energies from Yoga & Ayurveda to help others enhance their careers and personal growth.
