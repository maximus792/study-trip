# Bloomberg & Apple TV — 2 Minute Drill

[[3doshas]] was selected as a global finalist on the "2 Minute Drill" pitch show, airing on [[bloomberg]] TV and [[apple-tv]]. [[sachin-syal]] pitched on national television at age 15 — the world's first youngest finalist ever on the show.

## Judges

The judges included self-made multi-millionaires and billionaires:
- Janice Bryant Howroyd
- Rory Cutaia
- Jayson Waller
- David Meltzer

## Significance

This was a major visibility milestone for [[3doshas]], bringing the brand to a national television audience. The appearance is referenced prominently on the [[3doshas]] website and in [[sachin-syal]]'s biography. [[sachin-syal]] also won the [[ja-worldwide]] Impact Award in connection with this recognition.
