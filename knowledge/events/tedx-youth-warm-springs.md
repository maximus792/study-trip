# TEDxYouth@WarmSprings — Sachin Syal Talk

[[sachin-syal]] delivered the talk "How To Find Your Passion and Know Your Purpose" at age 13, making him one of the world's youngest TEDx speakers.

## Details

speaker:: [[sachin-syal]]
hosted_by:: [[tedx]]
location:: 47370 Warm Springs Blvd, Fremont, California
format:: Virtual

## Talk Summary

Sachin discussed how to find your passion which will reveal your purpose, drawing on his personal experience of discovering entrepreneurship at a young age — from selling candy in elementary school to making five figures on eBay by 13. He wanted to help others find their passion when they are young.

## Significance

This was a milestone event for both [[sachin-syal]] personally and [[3doshas]] as a brand, establishing Sachin's credibility as a young thought leader and speaker.
