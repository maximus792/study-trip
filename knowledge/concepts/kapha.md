# Kapha (Water)

One of the three doshas in the [[3doshas-methodology]]. Represents the energy of stability, nurturing, and endurance.

## Characteristics

part_of:: [[3doshas-methodology]]
element:: Water

Kapha is associated with stability, strength, and compassion. In the [[3doshas]] coaching framework, individuals with dominant Kapha energy tend toward:

- **Strengths**: Reliability, patience, loyalty, empathy, steady execution, team building
- **Challenges**: Resistance to change, complacency, difficulty with initiative, attachment
- **Career alignment**: Roles requiring relationship building, management, operations, and steady environments

## In Ayurvedic Tradition

Kapha governs structure, lubrication, and cohesion in body and mind. It is composed of the elements of water and earth. When balanced, Kapha promotes love, calmness, and forgiveness; when imbalanced, it can produce attachment and possessiveness.

## Application in Career Coaching

The [[career-alignment-blueprint]] helps Kapha-dominant individuals leverage their natural stability and relationship skills while developing initiative and embracing beneficial change.
