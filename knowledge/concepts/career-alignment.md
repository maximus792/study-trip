# Career Alignment

The central goal of [[3doshas]]' coaching practice: aligning an individual's career choices with their natural mind-body energy composition (doshas) to achieve peak performance and fulfillment.

## Core Idea

part_of:: [[3doshas-methodology]]

People perform at their best when their professional roles align with their natural energies. By understanding one's dominant dosha ([[vata]], [[pitta]], or [[kapha]]), individuals can:

- Choose roles that leverage their innate strengths
- Mitigate weaknesses by understanding their energy patterns
- Build sustainable career paths that avoid burnout
- Make transitions that feel natural rather than forced

## The Three-Step Journey

1. **[[career-alignment-blueprint]]** — Self-guided diagnostic to identify strengths, challenges, and a 30-day action plan
2. **[[career-alignment-call]]** — Guided conversation to discuss patterns and next moves
3. **[[one-on-one-coaching]]** — High-touch support to implement aligned changes

## For Teams

When teams harness diverse dosha strengths collectively, they innovate, execute, and sustain momentum. [[corporate-workshops]] help leadership teams understand how different dosha compositions complement each other.
