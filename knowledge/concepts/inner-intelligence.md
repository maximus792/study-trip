# Inner Intelligence

A core concept in the [[3doshas-methodology]] referring to each person's innate wisdom about their optimal path and natural strengths.

## Definition

part_of:: [[3doshas-methodology]]
used_by:: [[3doshas]]

Inner intelligence is the idea that every individual carries built-in knowledge about their unique strengths and ideal direction. The [[3doshas-methodology]] helps people access and trust this intelligence through understanding their dosha composition ([[vata]], [[pitta]], [[kapha]]).

## Application

Rather than imposing external career frameworks, the [[career-alignment-blueprint]] works to uncover what the individual already knows at a deeper level about where they will thrive. This aligns with the Ayurvedic principle that the body-mind system contains its own intelligence for healing and optimization.

## Connection to Coaching

When leaders understand and intentionally leverage their inner intelligence through the lens of the three doshas, they can build teams that innovate, execute, and sustain momentum — creating healthier, more effective collaboration.
