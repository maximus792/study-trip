# HyperPersonalization

A key principle in the [[3doshas-methodology]] that treats each individual as a "segment of one" — rejecting one-size-fits-all approaches to career development and leadership coaching.

## Definition

part_of:: [[3doshas-methodology]]
used_by:: [[3doshas]]

HyperPersonalization in the 3Doshas context means that every coaching engagement is tailored to the individual's unique dosha composition ([[vata]], [[pitta]], [[kapha]]), career history, and aspirations. No two blueprints are alike.

## The "Segment of One" Philosophy

Traditional career coaching often groups people into broad categories. The [[3doshas-methodology]] rejects this, using the [[career-alignment-blueprint]] to create a profile that is unique to each person's mind-body energy composition, resulting in highly specific career guidance and action plans.

## Application

- The [[career-alignment-blueprint]] generates a personalized profile
- Coaching sessions build on individual dosha patterns
- Corporate workshops reveal how diverse dosha compositions strengthen teams
- Action plans are tailored to individual energy patterns and career goals
