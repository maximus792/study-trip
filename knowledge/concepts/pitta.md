# Pitta (Fire)

One of the three doshas in the [[3doshas-methodology]]. Represents the energy of transformation, drive, and intensity.

## Characteristics

part_of:: [[3doshas-methodology]]
element:: Fire

Pitta is associated with transformation, ambition, and intellect. In the [[3doshas]] coaching framework, individuals with dominant Pitta energy tend toward:

- **Strengths**: Strong leadership, sharp intellect, decisiveness, goal-orientation, competitive drive
- **Challenges**: Tendency toward burnout, impatience, perfectionism, conflict
- **Career alignment**: Roles requiring leadership, strategy, analysis, and competitive environments

## In Ayurvedic Tradition

Pitta governs digestion, metabolism, and transformation in body and mind. It is composed of the elements of fire and water. When balanced, Pitta promotes understanding and intelligence; when imbalanced, it can produce anger and jealousy.

## Application in Career Coaching

The [[career-alignment-blueprint]] helps Pitta-dominant individuals channel their natural drive into sustainable leadership while managing tendencies toward overwork and burnout.
