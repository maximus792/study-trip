# 3Doshas Methodology

The science-based coaching framework developed by [[sumeet-syal]] and [[nitu-syal]] at [[3doshas]], blending timeless wisdom from Yoga & Ayurveda with modern coaching principles.

## Core Principles

developed_by:: [[sumeet-syal]]
developed_by:: [[nitu-syal]]
used_by:: [[3doshas]]

The methodology reveals genetic markers of an individual's mind-body makeup across three main energies known as doshas:

- **[[vata]]** (Air) — Movement, creativity, flexibility
- **[[pitta]]** (Fire) — Transformation, drive, intensity
- **[[kapha]]** (Water) — Stability, nurturing, endurance

## How It Works

1. **Assessment** — Identifies an individual's unique mind-body energy composition
2. **Blueprint** — The [[career-alignment-blueprint]] maps strengths to career paths
3. **Alignment** — Personalized coaching aligns natural energies with professional goals
4. **Optimization** — Teams learn to harness diverse dosha strengths collectively

## Philosophical Foundation

In Ayurvedic philosophy, every person carries a unique combination of the three doshas. When people align their career choices with their dosha composition, they enhance their strengths and mitigate weaknesses, leading to greater success and fulfillment.

The methodology applies [[inner-intelligence]] — the idea that each person has innate wisdom about their optimal path — and [[hyperpersonalization]] — treating each client as a "segment of one."

## Applications

- Executive coaching and leadership development
- Team building and corporate culture alignment
- Career transition and job placement
- Student mentorship and career guidance
- Workshop facilitation at corporations and universities
