# The Three Doshas

The foundational concept from Ayurvedic philosophy upon which [[3doshas]] and the [[3doshas-methodology]] are built. The three doshas are the three main energies that make up every individual's unique mind-body composition.

## The Three Energies

- **[[vata]]** (Air/Space) — The energy of movement, creativity, and flexibility
- **[[pitta]]** (Fire/Water) — The energy of transformation, drive, and intensity
- **[[kapha]]** (Water/Earth) — The energy of stability, nurturing, and endurance

In traditional Ayurveda, the doshas govern physical health, mental tendencies, and emotional patterns.
