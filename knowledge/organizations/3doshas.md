# 3Doshas

Award-winning lifestyle coaching company transforming lives through core principles inspired by Yoga & Ayurveda. Headquartered in Fremont, California. Founded in 2020.

## Overview

founded_by:: [[sumeet-syal]]
co_founded_by:: [[nitu-syal]]
co_founded_by:: [[sachin-syal]]
co_founded_by:: [[sujay-syal]]

3Doshas is a family-founded coaching & consulting company that blends the timeless wisdom of ancient Ayurvedic science with modern coaching principles. Their science-based methodology reveals genetic markers of mind-body makeup across three main energies known as doshas: [[vata]], [[pitta]], and [[kapha]].

## Mission

People perform at their best when they align with their natural energies — their "doshas" — and when teams harness those diverse strengths collectively. 3Doshas offers leaders a fresh, actionable framework for growth.
